# Market validation protocol (before code investment)

## Hypotheses
H1. Solo professionals lose paid time due to unpriced scope creep during fixed-fee projects.
H2. Their current solution (WhatsApp, email, docs, PM tools) causes enough friction to adopt a new tool.
H3. Sending a clear approval link improves agreement without damaging customer relationships.
H4. At least a subset will actually pay for the solution.

## Interview sample
Target 15–20 people across web dev, design, editing and marketing. At least 5 should have worked fixed-price projects recently; recruit outside friends as well. This is an initial qualitative exploration, not a representative study.

## Interview script (no pitching before examples)
1. Tell me about your last fixed-fee project. What was agreed initially?
2. What changed after work started? Can you show a redacted example?
3. What did you do when the client asked for more? What did you charge?
4. Estimate unpaid extra hours in the last 3 months; what supporting records exist?
5. What existing tool or habit handles the problem today?
6. Have you ever sent a separate quote/approval link? Why or why not?
7. Who decides on extra work; how do you verify their authorization?
8. What would make you distrust a proposed approval link?
9. Have you paid for a solution to this problem? How much and for how long?
10. After neutral clickable prototype, would you start a paid pilot now? Record actual behavior, not a polite yes.

## Experiments
- Unbranded clickable mock: baseline project -> extra request -> link -> acceptance.
- Concise landing page and direct outreach; track actual signups and objections.
- Concierge pilot: manually generate a scope-change document and send via WhatsApp for a user, without storing extra personal data.
- Pricing test: monthly subscription vs fixed number of change approvals; track paid conversion.

## Gates
**Discovery gate:** repeated specific unpaid-work cases from multiple independent interviewees AND obvious inadequacy of current habit.
**Prototype gate:** at least 5 target professionals complete a workflow without coaching; client reviewers understand price and effect.
**Commercial gate:** aim for >=3 genuine paid pilot commitments from qualified prospects (not friends promising abstract interest).
If gates fail, revise segment/pricing/problem or stop; do not silently code past validation.

## Evidence hygiene
Keep dated anonymized notes and interview counts. Do not present Reddit posts, estimates, downloads or self-reports as proof of viable paid demand.

## Privacy
Get consent before recording interviews; redact names, client messages, phone numbers and project contents. Do not upload client chat screenshots without permission.
